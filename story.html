Our story

Adornment is an old language.

A grandmother's bangles. A bride's rani haar. Jhumkas borrowed for Eid and never quite returned. In South Asian homes, jewelry carries memory as much as it carries light.

Sharif Adornment brings that tradition to Queens — jewelry and accessories chosen piece by piece for weddings, festivals and every celebration in between, and delivered to your door.

Mehndi. Nikah. Eid. Every celebration deserves its glow.
