Questions

Good to know.

Can't find what you're looking for? Message us — we're happy to help.

How do I order?

Tap “Ask about this piece” on anything you like, message us on Messenger, or call or text. We'll confirm availability, price and delivery with you directly.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery and contactless delivery are available. Ask us about timing for your area and your event date.

Can I see pieces before I order?

Yes — message us on Messenger and we'll send more photos and videos of any piece.

Why does it say “Price on request”?

Many pieces are sets or one-of-a-kind. Message us and we'll share the price straight away.

Can I rent a piece?

Some pieces can be rented for an event — they're marked “For rent”. Ask us about dates and terms.

Can I return or exchange a piece?

Please ask us before you order and we'll explain the options for your piece.

How should I care for my jewelry?

Keep it dry and away from perfume, lotion and hairspray. Put it on last and take it off first. Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing, and store each piece separately in a pouch or box so stones and pearls don't scratch.

Can you help me style a look?

Of course. Send us a photo of your outfit and the occasion, and we'll suggest a set, bangles and a potli to complete it.
